BUILT AROUND USVI

Private care, planned responsibly.

A villa in St. John is different from a St. Thomas resort or a St. Croix residence. We take the location and professional requirements seriously, confirming eligible local providers and property rules before accepting any request.

For villas, share the full address and gate details. For hotels, confirm outside-provider permission. For yachts, give the vessel name, marina or anchorage, and safe access arrangements.

USVI massage therapy is regulated. No appointment or therapist assignment is offered as confirmed until applicable local professional registration, business licensing, permits and property access are verified.
