USVI · OUR APPROACH

A simpler way to request private massage.

Tranquilitas is a Bahamas-founded wellness business coordinating destination-specific massage requests.

OPERATORS FIRST

Private treatments, without pretending every location is the same.

Tranquilitas Day Spa is based in Nassau, The Bahamas. Our regional approach began with the practical realities of coordinating professional massage: a real guest, an exact location, a suitable treatment space and a therapist who can actually attend.

We use a separate USVI website because guests searching for massage in U.S. Virgin Islands should find information about the island they are visiting, not a long regional dropdown. Locally compliant appointments across three distinct islands are part of that local context.

What the concierge actually reviews

  • Your chosen service and whether the requested appointment can be staffed locally.
  • Property access, location-specific travel and suitable massage-table space.
  • Any local qualifications, permits or professional rules applying to the service.
  • Final rates and whether special travel, marina or outdoor arrangements add charges.

What we do not promise

We do not publish invented guest testimonials, claim each property is an existing partner, or assume local therapists are available in every location at every time. A booking request is not a confirmed reservation. Payment is arranged only after the details have been reviewed.

USVI massage therapy is regulated. No appointment or therapist assignment is offered as confirmed until applicable local professional registration, business licensing, permits and property access are verified.
