What to expect from deep tissue massage

Deep tissue work involves slower, more focused pressure. It should never feel like a contest; tell the therapist if you want an adjustment. We arrange requests for guests in U.S. Virgin Islands who prefer a private, location-aware booking experience.

Whether you are returning from a beach day, an island drive or time on the water, the most comfortable appointment begins with the right space. Give us a quiet room, stable floor, parking and any building access instructions. The concierge checks coverage before a time is confirmed.

Who is this treatment suited to?

Guests who prefer firmer pressure and focused attention to ordinary muscle tightness. If you have an injury, significant swelling, unexplained pain, severe sunburn or feel unwell, obtain appropriate medical advice before arranging massage. Pressure and technique should always be discussed with the therapist.

Planning an appointment in USVI

Locally compliant appointments across three distinct islands shape the logistics of a local wellness request. Hotel policies, distances between areas and access to private residences or vessels can vary significantly across U.S. Virgin Islands. Please describe where you are actually staying.

  • Villa: share its name, address, gate code and a contact who can approve a private treatment room.
  • Hotel or resort: confirm whether external providers are allowed and whether the room safely fits a professional table.
  • Yacht: include the vessel, marina or anchorage, captain's approval and whether safe boarding is possible.
Local professional requirements: We do not assign or confirm a USVI appointment until applicable professional registration, business permits and local provider eligibility have been verified. See USVI Department of Health guidance.
