Frequently asked questions

Booking details for private massage enquiries in U.S. Virgin Islands.

THE PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Know before you request.

Can I book a massage at my hotel?

You can request hotel or resort massage where the property permits outside providers and the room can safely accommodate a professional table. Confirm the hotel's rules before requesting.

Do you offer mobile massage at villas?

Yes, villa and private-residence appointments can be requested in the listed areas, subject to therapist and property confirmation. Provide the full address, gate instructions and an appropriate treatment room.

Can we request couples massage?

Couples sessions can be requested. Simultaneous treatments require two locally available therapists, two tables and enough private space. Each person may choose their own preferred pressure.

Are beach treatments available?

The current site does not offer a beach massage booking product. Ask about suitable private indoor treatment spaces and local professional compliance instead.

Is the displayed price final?

No. Published amounts are guide rates from the regional service catalogue. The final amount, travel requirements and any special setup costs are confirmed before payment.

How far in advance should I request?

Request as early as practical. Therapist coverage, travel time and special access may require advance coordination. A last-minute request is not guaranteed.

Do you work with yacht guests?

Yacht enquiries should include the vessel's name, exact marina or anchorage, captain's contact, safe boarding method and suitable onboard or approved shoreside treatment space.

Is a submitted form a confirmed booking?

No. We send a confirmation only after local staffing, property access, provider requirements and pricing have been reviewed. You are not charged merely for enquiring.

What if my hotel refuses outside therapists?

We cannot override a property's rules. We may discuss a different suitable private setting if permitted and locally available, but we will not promise access without approval.

What happens if the therapist is unavailable?

We will explain that availability is not confirmed and discuss alternatives where possible. No payment or reservation is implied by the initial request.
